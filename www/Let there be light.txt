𝐋𝐄𝐓 𝐓𝐇𝐄𝐑𝐄 𝐁𝐄 𝐋𝐈𝐆𝐇𝐓 
𝐴𝑢𝑡ℎ𝑜𝑟:	𝑅𝑒𝑔𝑖𝑛𝑎𝑙𝑑 𝐻𝑒𝑏𝑒𝑟 (1823)

1.Holy, holy, holy! 
  Lord God Almighty!
  Early in the morning our
  song shall rise to thee.
  Holy, holy, holy! 
  merciful and mighty!
  God in three Persons, 
  blessed Trinity!

2.Holy, holy, holy! all 
  the saints adore thee,
  Casting down their golden
  crowns around the glassy sea.
  Cherubim and seraphim, 
  falling down before thee,
  Who was and is and
  evermore shall be.

3.Holy, Holy, Holy! though 
  the darkness hide thee,
  Though the eye of sinful
  man thy glory may not see,
  Only thou art holy; there
  is none beside thee,
  Perfect in pow'r, 
  in love, and purity.

4.Holy, holy, holy! 
  Lord God Almighty!
  All thy works shall praise
  thy name, in earth, and sky,
  and sea;Holy, holy, holy! 
  merciful and mighty!
  God in three Persons,
  blessed Trinity.
