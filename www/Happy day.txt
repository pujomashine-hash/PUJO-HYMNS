𝑯𝑨𝑷𝑷𝒀 𝑫𝑨𝒀 
𝑨𝒖𝒕𝒉𝒐𝒓:	𝑷𝒉𝒊𝒍𝒊𝒑 𝑫𝒐𝒅𝒅𝒓𝒊𝒅𝒈𝒆 (1750)

1.O happy day, that
  fixed my choice
  on Thee, my Savior
  and my God!
  Well may this glowing
  heart rejoice,
  and tell its pratures all abroad.

2.'Tis done; the great
   transaction's done!
  I am my Lord's,
  and He is mine.
  He drew me, and
  I followed on,
  charmed to confess
  the voice divine.

3 Now rest, my long
  divided heart;
  fixed on this blissful
  center, rest.
  Here have I found
  a nobler part;
  here heav'nly pleasures
  sure fill my breast.

4.High heav'n, that
  heard the solemn vow,
  that vow renewed
   shall daily hear,
  till in life's 
  latest hour I bow,
  and bless in death
  a bond so dear.
