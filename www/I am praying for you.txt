𝑰 𝑨𝑴 𝑷𝑹𝑨𝒀𝑰𝑵𝑮 𝑭𝑶𝑹 𝒀𝑶𝑼 
𝑨𝒖𝒕𝒉𝒐𝒓:	𝑺𝒂𝒎𝒖𝒆𝒍 𝑶'𝑴𝒂𝒍𝒍𝒆𝒚 𝑪𝒍𝒖𝒇𝒇 (1866)


1.I have a Savior, 
  He’s pleading in glory,
  A dear, loving Savior, 
  though earth-friends be few;
  And now He is watching
  in tenderness o’er me,
  But O, that my Savior
  were your Savior, too!

Refrain:
For you I am praying,
For you I am praying,
For you I am praying,
I’m praying for you.

2.I have a Father; 
  to me He has given
  A hope for eternity, 
  blessed and true;
  And soon will He call me 
  to meet Him in heaven,
  But O, that He’d let me 
  bring you with me, too!
 [Refrain]

3.I have a robe, ’tis 
  resplendent in whiteness,
  Awaiting in glory
  my wondering view;
  O, when I receive it all
  shining in brightness,
  Dear friend, could I see
  you receiving one, too! 
[Refrain]

4.When He has found you, 
  tell others the story,
  That my loving Savior 
  is your Savior, too;
  Then pray that your Savior
  may bring them to glory,
  And prayer will be answered- 
  ’twas answered for you! 
[Refrain]
