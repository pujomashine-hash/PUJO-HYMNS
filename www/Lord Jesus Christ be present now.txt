𝑳𝑶𝑹𝑫 𝑱𝑬𝑺𝑼𝑺 𝑪𝑯𝑹𝑰𝑺𝑻, 𝑩𝑬 𝑷𝑹𝑬𝑺𝑬𝑵𝑻 𝑵𝑶𝑾
𝑪𝒐𝒎𝒑𝒐𝒔𝒆𝒓: 𝑾𝒊𝒍𝒉𝒆𝒍𝒎 𝑰𝑰 𝒐𝒇 𝑺𝒂𝒙𝒆-𝑾𝒆𝒊𝒎𝒂𝒓 (1648)
𝑻𝒓𝒂𝒏𝒔𝒍𝒂𝒕𝒊𝒐𝒏: 𝑪𝒂𝒕𝒉𝒆𝒓𝒊𝒏𝒆 𝑾𝒊𝒏𝒌𝒘𝒐𝒓𝒕𝒉 (1863)

1. Lord Jesus Christ, be present now,
   Our hearts in true devotion bow;
   Thy Spirit send with light divine,
   And let Thy truth within us shine.

2. Unseal our lips to sing Thy praise,
   Our souls to Thee in worship raise;
   Make strong our faith, increase our light,
   That we may know Thy name aright.

3. Until we join the hosts above,
   Who echo Thy redeeming love,
   And see Thee face to face in light,
   Enthroned in glory, majesty, and might.

4. Glory to God the Father, Son,
   And Holy Spirit, Three in One!
   To Thee, O blessed Trinity,
   Be praise throughout eternity!
