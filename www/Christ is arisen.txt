𝑻𝒆𝒙𝒕: 𝑮𝒆𝒓𝒎𝒂𝒏 (12𝒕𝒉 𝑪𝒆𝒏𝒕𝒖𝒓𝒚) / 𝑴𝒂𝒓𝒕𝒊𝒏 𝑳𝒖𝒕𝒉𝒆𝒓 (1524)
𝑻𝒓𝒂𝒏𝒔𝒍𝒂𝒕𝒊𝒐𝒏: 𝑾. 𝑮𝒖𝒔𝒕𝒂𝒗𝒆 𝑷𝒐𝒍𝒂𝒄𝒌 (1941)

1. Christ is arisen
   From the grave's dark prison.
   We now rejoice with gladness;
   Christ will solace all our sadness.
   Kyrieleis.

2. Had He not arisen,
   In death's gloomy prison
   We all would be forsaken;
   But now is the world awaken.
   Kyrieleis.

3. Alleluia, 
   Alleluia, 
   Alleluia!
   We now rejoice with gladness;
   Christ will solace all our sadness.
   Kyrieleis.
