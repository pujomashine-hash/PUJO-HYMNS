𝐈'𝐌 𝐍𝐎𝐓 𝐀𝐒𝐇𝐀𝐌𝐄𝐃 𝐓𝐎 𝐎𝐖𝐍 𝐌𝐘 𝐋𝐎𝐑𝐃 
𝐴𝑢𝑡ℎ𝑜𝑟:	𝐼𝑠𝑎𝑎𝑐 𝑊𝑎𝑡𝑡𝑠 (1709)

1.I'm not ashamed 
  to own my Lord,
  or to defend his cause,
  maintain the honor
  of his Word,
  the glory of his cross.

2.Jesus, my God! 
  I know his name,
  his name is all my trust;
  nor will he put
   my soul to shame,
  nor let my hope be lost.

3.Firm as his throne 
  his promise stands,
  and he can well secure
  what I've committed 
  to his hands
  'til the decisive hour.

4.Then will he own 
  my worthless name
  before his Father's face,
  and in the new Jerusalem
  appoint my soul a place.
